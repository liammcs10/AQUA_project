### Phase response Curve findings

Study on how the autapse changes the phase response curve properties of the neuron.

#### 20 Nov. 2026

Parameters: 
RS - integrator
f = 0:400:10
I_0 = [100, 150, 200, 250]
step perturbation, height = 100 pA, duration = 0.01 ms

Findings:
    - autapse is most effective at lower firing rates. 
    - Autapses pushes the neuron's sensitivity to inputs early in the ISI, immediately 
    after the spike (consistent with enhanced coincidence detection).
