SOME GENERAL FINDINGS:

RS NEURONS:
- For the RS neuron, the addition of an autapse may serve 
to transition between RS and IB type firing. 
- The autapse produces a 'bursting' regime in the steady steady_state
- The autapse produces a region of high rate of change in the initial firing rate 
as a function of driving current. 
- These results seem robust for different RS models (integrator/resonator)
- AUTAPTIC RS IS VERY SIMILAR TO NON-AUTAPTIC IB (ADJUSTED FOR DRIVING CURRENT).

* Attentional mechanisms?

IB NEURON:
- The autapse extends and drastically increases the average firing rate of the neuron 
in the bursting region in the F-I curve. 
- The derivative of the initial firing rate curve doesn't seem to change with the 
autapse, the curve is only shifted to the left (lower I).
- Could the IB neuron then help maintain bursting/oscillations?
- There might be a strange attractor formed for a specific set of autapse Parameters
    (same values as the RS bifurcation...)
